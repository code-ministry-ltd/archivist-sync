# Changelog

All notable changes to **Archivist Sync** are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-01

### Added

- **The first release** (The Archivist's spec 0012):
  - one-way sync of the folders you choose, with a settings page in your browser;
  - one sign-in per computer, approved in your archive;
  - start at login on macOS, Windows and Linux;
  - the deletion guard;
  - signed self-updates;
  - commands for people and AI assistants, with `--json`.
